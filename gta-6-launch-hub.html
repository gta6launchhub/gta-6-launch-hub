Both editions went on sale on the PlayStation Store, the Xbox Store and at retailers — and pre-orders reportedly passed one billion dollars before launch week even began.

Rockstar moved pre-load forward from its original November 12 slot. Anyone with a digital pre-order can download the full game now and simply wait for the unlock.

The physical Standard Edition arrived at retailers — a box containing a download code, not a disc — so boxed buyers could start their pre-load too.

04

Pre-load: be ready at midnight

At up to 212 GB, this is not a download you want to start at 11:58 PM. Pre-load is already open — here is how to finish it on each console.

PlayStation 5 PS5

  1. Find the game in your Game Library, under the Purchased tab — it appears there as soon as a pre-order goes through.
  2. Select GTA VI and choose Download. Pre-load opened at midnight local time on November 10.
  3. Keep rest mode download-friendly: in Settings, allow the console to stay connected to the internet in rest mode so the download finishes unattended.
  4. At midnight on Nov 19 the licence activates and the game icon unlocks — no second download needed.

Xbox Series X|S Xbox

  1. Open My games & apps and find Grand Theft Auto VI in your library after pre-ordering.
  2. Choose Install. Xbox pre-load opened November 10 at 12:00 AM GMT (7:00 PM ET on November 9).
  3. Bought the boxed copy? Redeem the code inside the box, then install the same way — boxed-code pre-load began November 12.
  4. At midnight on Nov 19 your copy unlocks automatically; a small day-one update may still apply.

What about PC?

The most-searched GTA 6 question after the release date itself — and the one with the shortest honest answer.

No PC version has been announced.

Rockstar has given no PC release date, no PC price and no system requirements — there is no Steam page and nothing to pre-order. Every official listing names PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the only launch platforms. Anything you see elsewhere claiming a PC download, a PC key or a "leaked build" is not from Rockstar: treat it as a scam risk, not a shortcut.

When — and if — a PC version is announced, it will appear on Rockstar's official GTA VI page and Newswire first. Until it does, this section stays exactly as it is: unannounced.

06

Launch-night checklist

Five small things to sort out before Thursday, so midnight is spent playing — not downloading, updating or hunting for cables.

  • Finish the pre-load
    Start it today if you haven't. Up to 212 GB takes hours even on fast connections, and launch-night store traffic will not make it quicker.
  • Clear 220 GB of storage
    Delete or archive games you are not playing. Leave headroom for the day-one update on top of the base install.
  • Update the console itself
    Install any pending system software update now, and charge your controller — a firmware prompt at 12:01 AM is a rite of passage nobody enjoys.
  • Check your edition and bonus
    Buying before November 20 keeps the Vintage Vice City Pack included. Digital buyers: note the GTA+ month must be claimed by March 31, 2027.
  • Go spoiler-quiet
    Players in Auckland unlock a full 23 hours before Honolulu — and 21 hours before Los Angeles. Mute the obvious keywords on social media until you have played the opening yourself.
07

Quick answers

What time exactly can I start playing?

At 12:00 AM on Thursday, November 19, 2026, in your local time zone, per the console store listings. If you pre-loaded, the game unlocks in place — expect at most a small day-one update before you can play.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not that Rockstar has announced. There is no PC release date, no Steam page and no PC pre-order. Launch platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and there are no PS4 or Xbox One versions either.

Does the boxed copy contain a disc?

No. Rockstar's own store wording is explicit: the physical Standard Edition box contains a download code only — "a disc will not be included in the box." The code lets you pre-load from November 12.

Standard or Ultimate — and can I change my mind?

Both editions are the same full game. Ultimate adds 16 exclusive items — vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops — that unlock as the story progresses, for $20 / £20 / €20 / ₹1,500 more. If you buy Standard first, the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is sold separately, so you can add the extras later at the same difference.

How much storage do I need?

Rockstar puts the install at up to 212 GB, depending on your console. Free up a little more than that — around 220 GB — so the day-one update and save data have room too.

What do I get for buying early?

Pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack at no extra cost, and digital purchases also include one month of GTA+, claimable until March 31, 2027 on the buying account. There is no early-access play window — everyone starts at midnight.
