01

The only number Rockstar has given

There is exactly one on-record figure for how long GTA 6 takes — and it comes from the person who made the game.

Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told The New York Times (August 27, 2026, the day of the Extended Look) that his own playthrough took about 80 hours. The run covered the main story plus “some optional goals with narrative ramifications” — side objectives that change how the story plays out. It was finished in February 2026, and it was not his first playthrough: he called it his “last playthrough”, so he already knew the game well.

That is the one on-record figure. Rockstar has published no official runtime, no mission count and no completionist estimate — and the launch trailer in October added nothing on length.

02

04

Why your playthrough will differ

Story length in GTA 6 depends on how you play — and the game gives you more reasons to linger than any Rockstar game before it.

Reports describe a campaign split into chapters, with choices that can bend the story — and some story progress requires earning real money through robberies and car theft, so a careful player and a rushing player will finish hours apart. Exploring Leonida, side activities and collectibles all add time on top of the story.

Other sites estimate 100+ hours for a story-plus-exploring run and 150–200+ hours for completionists — those are their estimates, not Rockstar’s, and no official definition of “100%” exists yet. The only honest advice: budget at least 80 hours for the story, and expect more if you linger.

05

Story length, answered

The short version of everything above.

How long is GTA 6’s story?

About 80 hours, by the only on-record figure: Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told The New York Times his own playthrough took about 80 hours, covering the main story and some optional goals.

Is 80 hours the main story only?

No. The run included the main story plus “optional goals with narrative ramifications”. Rockstar has not given a pure main-story-only figure.

How long does 100% completion take?

Not published. Other sites estimate 150–200+ hours — those are their estimates, and Rockstar has not defined what “100%” means for GTA 6 yet.

Is GTA 6 longer than Red Dead Redemption 2?

On the numbers available, yes: Nelson’s ~80 hours against RDR2’s ~51-hour main story (HowLongToBeat). But the comparison is not like-for-like, because Nelson’s run included optional content.

How long is GTA 5 for comparison?

About 32 hours for the main story and about 51.5 hours with extras, by HowLongToBeat player averages.
