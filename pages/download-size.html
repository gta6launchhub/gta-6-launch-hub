How big is the GTA 6 download?

Up to 212 GB — the largest launch download in the series' history, and not one to start at 11:58 PM.

The exact size varies a little by console and by region. "Up to 212 GB" is the published ceiling — treat it as the number to plan around, not a worst case to hope under.

03

How to free 220 GB

Two consoles, two sets of moves. Do this before launch night, not during it.

PlayStation 5 PS5

  1. Delete games you are not playing: Settings > Storage > Console Storage > Games and Apps. Sort by size — one or two big games usually covers 220 GB.
  2. Move PS4 games to a USB drive: PS4 games can run from USB extended storage, so move them off the internal drive and keep playing them there.
  3. Park PS5 games on USB too: PS5 games can be stored on a USB drive, but they must be copied back to internal storage to play — useful for games you will return to later.
  4. Clear the capture gallery: Media Gallery videos and screenshots add up quietly. Delete clips you have already shared or backed up.

Xbox Series X|S Xbox

  1. Uninstall what you finished: My games & apps > Manage > Free up space shows your biggest games first.
  2. Move games to an external USB drive: Series X|S games can be stored on USB, but they must be on internal storage — or the official expansion card — to actually play.
  3. Drop add-ons you don't use: 4K asset packs and language packs for games you still play can free tens of gigabytes each.
  4. Check for the expansion card option: if your drive is nearly full and you play a lot, the official Storage Expansion Card is the only way to add fast, playable space.
04

Pre-load, step by step

Pre-load is already open — finish it now and midnight is just an unlock, not a download.

PlayStation 5 PS5

  1. Find the game in your Game Library, under the Purchased tab — it appears there as soon as a pre-order goes through.
  2. Select GTA VI and choose Download. Pre-load opened at midnight local time on November 10.
  3. Keep rest mode download-friendly: in Settings, allow the console to stay connected to the internet in rest mode so the download finishes unattended.
  4. At midnight on Nov 19 the licence activates and the game icon unlocks — no second download needed.

Xbox Series X|S Xbox

  1. Open My games & apps and find Grand Theft Auto VI in your library after pre-ordering.
  2. Choose Install. Xbox pre-load opened November 10 at 12:00 AM GMT (7:00 PM ET on November 9).
  3. Bought the boxed copy? Redeem the code inside the box, then install the same way — boxed-code pre-load began November 12.
  4. At midnight on Nov 19 your copy unlocks automatically; a small day-one update may still apply.

06

Download questions, answered

Everything players ask about the 212 GB install.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Rockstar and PlayStation put the full install at up to 212 GB, depending on your console. Plan for the full figure — it varies a little by console and region, not by edition.

Do I need the full 212 GB free before downloading?

Yes — and a little more. Free up around 220 GB so the day-one update and your save data have room too. The console will refuse to start the download if the space is not there.

Can I pre-load GTA 6?

Yes. Digital pre-load opened November 10 on both consoles, and boxed-code pre-load began November 12. If you have not started it yet, start it now.

Is there a day-one update?

Expect a small one. Pre-loading gets you the full game; at the midnight unlock a small update may still apply before you can play.

Can I install GTA 6 on an external hard drive?

Not to play from. On PS5, PS5 games must live on internal storage to run — USB is storage only. On Xbox, Series X|S games must be on internal storage or the official expansion card to run. An external drive is still useful for parking other games to make room.

Does the boxed copy need a download too?

Yes. The box contains a download code, not a disc — so boxed buyers download the same 212 GB after redeeming the code.
