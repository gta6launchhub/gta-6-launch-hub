01

The story, in short

Rockstar's own summary, in our words: two partners, one job gone wrong, and a whole state against them.

Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. When an easy score goes wrong, they end up on the wrong side of the sunniest place in America, caught in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the entire state of Leonida — and the only way out is together.

That is the whole official plot so far. Who is behind the conspiracy, what the "easy score" was, and how it ends — Rockstar has not said. Everything else on this page comes from the trailers and Rockstar's character profiles, and anything beyond that is labelled as guesswork.

02

Lucia Caminos

The first face we ever saw of GTA 6 — in an orange jumpsuit, inside Leonida Penitentiary.

Trailer 1, from December 2023, opens on Lucia in prison. Her official profile fills in the outline: her father taught her to fight, and defending her family is what sent her to the penitentiary. She comes out determined to do things her way this time — only smart moves.

Trailer 2 shows her release, with Jason picking her up — and then the two of them planning jobs, handling a gun and driving getaways together. She wants a good life with Jason, whatever it takes. Lucia is the first woman to lead a Grand Theft Auto game.

03

Jason Duval

A man trying to stay out of trouble in a state that keeps pulling him back in.

Jason lives in the Leonida Keys and works for local operators. Brian Heder lets him live rent-free in one of his properties — in exchange for help with collections and shakedowns. Jason wants to change his life and stay out of trouble; the trailers make it clear that trouble finds him anyway.

Trailer 2 shows his quiet routine: working out on the beach, fixing up a house, buying beer, picking Lucia up from prison. The Launch Trailer from October 15, 2026 hints at a troubled past in the Army and a bad exit — but that is a reading of the footage, not a written backstory from Rockstar, so take it as a hint rather than a fact.

04

Playing as both

GTA 6 is a two-lead game — and the Extended Look from August 2026 showed how switching between them works.

The Extended Look showed the game switching from Jason to Lucia mid-chase — sometimes the switch is free, and sometimes the story forces it. The romance between the two is optional, according to Rob Nelson, co-head of Rockstar North.

Nelson also said his own playthrough took about 80 hours with some optional content included. Treat that as one developer's playthrough, not a promised length — your time will depend on how much of Leonida you explore.

07

Character questions, answered

The questions players ask most about Lucia, Jason and the cast.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval — two protagonists, partners in crime and in life, caught in a conspiracy that stretches across the state of Leonida.

Can you play as both Jason and Lucia?

Yes. The Extended Look showed the game switching between them mid-mission. Sometimes the switch is free, and sometimes the story forces it.

Is Lucia the first female protagonist in GTA?

Yes — Lucia is the first woman to lead a Grand Theft Auto game. Earlier games had playable women in multiplayer or as minor characters, but never as a lead.

Who voices Jason and Lucia?

Not announced. Rockstar has published no cast list, so every actor name you see online is unconfirmed.

Is the romance between Jason and Lucia forced?

No — according to Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson, the romance is optional.

How long is the GTA 6 story?

No official length has been announced. Rob Nelson said his own playthrough took about 80 hours with some optional content — that is one developer's playthrough, not a promised length.
