Launch & unlock

When the game starts, and why it starts at a different hour in every city.

What time exactly can I start playing?

At 12:00 AM on Thursday, November 19, 2026, in your local time zone, per the console store listings. If you pre-loaded, the game unlocks in place — expect at most a small day-one update before you can play.

What date does GTA 6 come out?

Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It was delayed twice before — from fall 2025, to May 26, 2026, and then to November 19.

Is there early access to GTA 6?

No. There is no early-access play window for any edition. Everyone starts at midnight local time on November 19.

Will GTA 6 unlock at the same time everywhere?

No. The game unlocks at midnight local time in each region, so Auckland players start a full 23 hours before Honolulu. See the unlock times table on the Launch Hub home page for 31 cities.

Platforms & PC

Where GTA 6 runs — and where it does not, yet.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not that Rockstar has announced. There is no PC release date, no Steam page and no PC pre-order. A GamesBeat report from October 13, 2026 points to the first quarter of 2027, citing four anonymous sources — but Rockstar has not confirmed it. Full detail: GTA 6 PC release date.

Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. GTA 6 is built for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There are no last-generation versions.

Is GTA 6 coming to Nintendo Switch?

Not announced. Rockstar has named only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as launch platforms.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC through cloud streaming at launch?

No. Xbox has clarified that GTA 6 will not stream to PC via cloud when the console version launches.

Editions & price

What the game costs, and which version to buy.

How much does GTA 6 cost?

The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 in the US. The same $20 gap applies in the UK (£69.99 / £89.99), the Eurozone (€79.99 / €99.99) and India (₹5,999 / ₹7,499). Full detail: editions compared.

Standard or Ultimate — which should I buy?

Both are the complete game. Ultimate adds 16 exclusive in-game items for $20 more. If you buy Standard first, the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is sold separately at the same difference — so you can decide later.

Does the boxed copy contain a disc?

No. The physical Standard Edition box contains a download code only — a disc is not included. The Ultimate Edition is digital only; there is no boxed version.

04

Download & pre-load

The 212 GB question, and how to be ready at midnight.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Rockstar and PlayStation put the full install at up to 212 GB, depending on your console. Free up around 220 GB so the day-one update and save data have room. Full detail: download size & storage guide.

Can I pre-load GTA 6?

Yes. Digital pre-load opened November 10 on both consoles, and boxed-code pre-load began November 12. If you have not started it yet, start it now — 212 GB takes hours even on fast connections.

How much storage do I need?

Around 220 GB free is the safe target. On a 512 GB Xbox Series S, that is most of the drive — check the storage guide for your console.

Is there a day-one update?

Expect a small one. Pre-loading gets you the full game; at the midnight unlock a small update may still apply before you can play.

Map & characters

Where the game is set, and who you play as.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In the fictional state of Leonida — Rockstar's take on Florida — with Vice City as the main city. Rockstar has officially named six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Full detail: the map of Leonida.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

No official figure exists. Rockstar has not published a map or a size, so any number you see is a fan estimate, not a fact.

Who are the main characters?

Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval — two protagonists, partners in crime and in life. Lucia is fresh out of prison; Jason lives in the Leonida Keys and wants a quiet life. Full detail: Lucia & Jason.

Can you play as both characters?

Yes. The Extended Look showed the game switching between Jason and Lucia mid-mission — sometimes the switch is free, and sometimes the story forces it.

Buying & bonuses

Pre-orders, bonuses and where to buy.

What do I get for buying early?

Pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack at no extra cost, and digital purchases also include one month of GTA+, claimable until March 31, 2027 on the buying account.

Where can I buy GTA 6?

On the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store, and at retailers for the boxed Standard Edition. For anything that decides a purchase — price, region, terms — the official store pages win; links are on the Launch Hub home page.

Is GTA 6 digital-only?

No — but nearly. The boxed Standard Edition exists, though it contains a download code rather than a disc, and the Ultimate Edition is digital only.
