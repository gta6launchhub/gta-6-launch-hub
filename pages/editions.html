The 16 Ultimate extras, explained

What the extra $20 actually buys — and what it does not.

The Ultimate Edition adds 16 exclusive items: vehicles, weapons, outfits and dedicated shops. They are in-game gear — not extra story missions, not a longer campaign, and not early access. They unlock gradually as the story progresses, so you will not have all 16 on day one.

The extras are exclusive to the Ultimate Edition and its Upgrade — they are not sold one by one. If you mostly care about the story, they change very little about your first playthrough. If you like collecting cars, clothes and weapons, they are the whole point of the extra $20.

04

Which edition should you buy?

Our honest take: there is no wrong answer, because the Upgrade means Standard is never a dead end.

Buy Standard if you just want the story, you are unsure about the extras, or you want the boxed copy — the Ultimate Edition has no boxed version. Buy Ultimate if you want the 16 extras from day one and $20 is worth it to you, and you are buying digital anyway. Either way, both editions are the complete game, and Standard can be upgraded later for the same $20 difference — so there is no reason to rush the decision.

05

Bonuses that apply to both editions

These do not depend on which edition you pick — only on when and how you buy.

  • Vintage Vice City Pack
    Included at no extra cost with either edition when you buy before November 20, 2026.
  • One month of GTA+
    Included with digital purchases of either edition. It must be claimed by March 31, 2027, on the account that bought the game.
  • No early access, either way
    Neither edition lets you play before midnight on November 19. Everyone starts at the same moment.
  • Boxed means a code, not a disc
    The physical Standard Edition box contains a download code only. There is no boxed Ultimate Edition at all.
06

Edition questions, answered

The questions players ask before paying $79.99 or $99.99.

Is the Ultimate Edition worth it?

It depends on what you value. The extra $20 buys 16 exclusive in-game items — vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops — that unlock as the story progresses. It does not buy extra story, extra missions or early access. If you like collecting, it is worth it; if you only care about the story, Standard is the same game.

Is there a physical Ultimate Edition?

No. The Ultimate Edition is digital only. The only boxed version is the Standard Edition — and even that box holds a download code, not a disc.

Can I upgrade from Standard to Ultimate later?

Yes. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade is sold separately (US$20 on the Xbox Store), so you can start with Standard and add the 16 extras later at the same difference.

Do both editions have the full story?

Yes. Both editions contain the complete single-player story. The Ultimate extras are gear, not content — nothing in the story is held back from Standard buyers.

What is the Vintage Vice City Pack?

A bonus pack included with pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026, with either edition. It is a nostalgia-themed set of in-game items — not a separate game or map.
