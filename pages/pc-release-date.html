01

The official position

The most-searched GTA 6 question after the release date itself — and the one with the shortest honest answer.

No PC version has been announced.

Rockstar has given no PC release date, no PC price and no system requirements — there is no Steam page, no Epic Games Store page and nothing to pre-order. Every official listing names PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the only launch platforms. Anything you see elsewhere claiming a PC download, a PC key or a "leaked build" is not from Rockstar: treat it as a scam risk, not a shortcut.

When — and if — a PC version is announced, it will appear on Rockstar's official GTA VI page and Newswire first. Until it does, this page stays exactly as it is: unannounced.

03

Why the Q1 2027 report matters — and its limits

The GamesBeat report is the best-sourced claim so far. It is still only a report.

On October 13, 2026, GamesBeat reported that Rockstar plans to release the PC version in the first quarter of 2027, citing four industry sources familiar with the studio's plans. Rockstar did not respond to a request for comment, and has said nothing about a PC version since.

The report names no month, no day, no price and no storefronts. The first quarter of 2027 runs from January 1 to March 31 — which would put a PC launch anywhere from 43 to 132 days after the console launch. If it happened, it would be the fastest PC port in Rockstar's modern history by a wide margin.

Treat any headline that presents Q1 2027 as confirmed with suspicion: right now it traces back to one report built on anonymous sources, not to an official announcement.

05

What the bosses have actually said

Two public statements, both careful — and neither one is a date.

Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick, speaking in May 2026, said that nearly all of the company's hit titles eventually reach every platform over time — while stressing that GTA 6 has been announced for console only so far. It is a hint about direction, not a commitment about timing.

And for PC players hoping to stream the console version at launch: Xbox's Matthew Ball has clarified that GTA 6 will not be available to stream to PC via cloud on launch day.

06

Don't get scammed

A game this wanted attracts fakes. Five rules that keep your PC and your wallet safe.

  • No PC download exists yet
    Any site offering a "GTA 6 PC download", a "PC key" or a "leaked build" is selling something that does not exist. Downloads from such sites are a classic malware trap.
  • No early-access PC keys exist
    There is no PC pre-order, so nobody has a PC key to sell you — at any price.
  • Trust two sources only
    Rockstar's official GTA VI page and the Rockstar Newswire. If a PC date is not there, it is not confirmed — wherever else you read it.
  • Check the date on the claim
    Old articles and recycled videos resurface as "news" every few months. A Q1 2027 claim from October 2026 is the newest solid report — anything older is not newer information.
07

PC questions, answered

The short version of everything above — plus the questions PC players keep asking.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not that Rockstar has announced. The game launches November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. A PC version has not been confirmed, denied, dated or priced.

When is the GTA 6 PC release date?

There is no official date. The only timing in circulation is a GamesBeat report from October 13, 2026 pointing to the first quarter of 2027 — January to March — citing four anonymous sources. Rockstar has not confirmed it.

Will GTA 6 be on Steam?

Unknown. No Steam page exists, and the GamesBeat report does not name any storefronts. If a PC version is announced, the storefronts will be part of that announcement.

What will the PC system requirements be?

Not announced. Any 'minimum specs' or 'recommended specs' you see today are guesses. Rockstar has published no PC requirements because it has announced no PC version.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC through cloud streaming at launch?

No. Xbox has clarified that GTA 6 will not stream to PC via cloud when the console version launches.

Should I buy a console just for GTA 6?

That depends on how long you are willing to wait. The reported PC window is Q1 2027, but it is unconfirmed — and past Rockstar games took 13 to 19 months to reach PC. If the report is wrong, the wait could be much longer.
