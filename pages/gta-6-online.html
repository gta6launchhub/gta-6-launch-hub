01

The short answer

No online multiplayer has been announced for Grand Theft Auto VI at launch.

Single-player only at launch.

Grand Theft Auto VI launches November 19, 2026 as a single-player game. Rockstar’s pre-order announcement (June 24, 2026) describes it as a game that “features a single-player experience”, and PlayStation’s official GTA VI FAQ answers the multiplayer question with one line: “Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience.” Both console stores list the game as single-player only. No online mode, co-op campaign or multiplayer lobby has been announced for launch day.

03

The 2027 reports — what they are and what they aren’t

Outside reporting points to 2027. None of it is an announcement.

Twitch chief executive Dan Clancy told Bloomberg (September 17, 2026) that he expects GTA 6 multiplayer “next year” — 2027. That is his expectation, not an announcement, and VGC noted it was unclear whether he was confirming timing or guessing.

Push Square reports that an updated version of GTA Online is expected sometime in 2027, possibly in the first quarter — again, a report, not a confirmation. And Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier has reported that Rockstar has no plans to talk about a new online mode soon.

One more reported detail: a Rockstar tribunal statement is said to have discussed a 32-player online format. That comes from a court filing, not from Rockstar’s marketing — treat it as a reported detail, not a feature list. The ESRB listing for GTA 6 also does not carry the “Users Interact” notice that GTA 5’s listing had for its online side, read by TechTimes (October 2, 2026) as another sign of no multiplayer at launch.

The honest answer to “when is GTA 6 Online coming out?” is: sometime after launch, most likely in 2027 according to outside reporting — but Rockstar has said nothing, so treat every date you see as a guess until Rockstar posts it.

04

Don’t confuse it with the GTA+ bonus

Digital pre-orders include a month of GTA+. That is a perk for the online game you can already play — not a GTA 6 mode.

Digital pre-orders include one month of GTA+, and GTA+ gives access to GTA Online benefits and the GTA+ Games Library. That is the existing GTA Online — the multiplayer mode of GTA 5 — not a GTA 6 mode.

The current GTA Online keeps running after GTA 6 launches, and Take-Two has said it will keep supporting it. Your GTA Online characters, money and progress stay in GTA 5’s online world: Rockstar has announced no transfer into GTA 6.

05

What it means if you’re buying for multiplayer

Four plain consequences of a single-player launch.

  • If you want multiplayer, wait
    There is nothing to play online in GTA 6 on launch day. The online game that exists on November 19 is the old one, on GTA 5.
  • There is no crossplay question yet
    There is no multiplayer to cross-play. When an online mode is announced, crossplay will be one of the first questions to ask.
  • No microtransactions at launch
    Take-Two expects no recurrent spending from the launch itself. That is a statement about the launch, not a promise that a later online mode would be free of them.
  • Watch the Newswire, not the rumours
    When Rockstar does announce an online mode, it will appear on the Newswire first. Until then, every date is a rumour.
06

GTA 6 Online, answered

The questions players actually ask about multiplayer.

Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

Not at launch. Rockstar describes Grand Theft Auto VI as a “single-player experience”, and PlayStation’s official FAQ says the same.

When is GTA 6 Online coming out?

There is no announced date. Twitch chief executive Dan Clancy told Bloomberg he expects multiplayer in 2027, but Rockstar has not confirmed any timing.

Will GTA Online shut down when GTA 6 launches?

No. Take-Two has said it will continue to support the current GTA Online after GTA VI’s release, with no end date given.

Does the GTA+ bonus include GTA 6 Online?

No. The one free month of GTA+ that comes with digital pre-orders covers the existing GTA Online (GTA 5) benefits — not a GTA 6 mode.

Is there crossplay in GTA 6?

There is no multiplayer at launch, so there is no crossplay.

Can I transfer my GTA Online character to GTA 6?

No transfer has been announced. Your GTA Online characters, money and progress stay in GTA 5’s online world.
