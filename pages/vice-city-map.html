The six regions Rockstar has named

02

Vice City, up close

Both trailers spend most of their time in the city — and the details add up to more than a skyline.

What the trailers show: Downtown high-rises and highway signage, the Ocean Beach strip and the Ocean View Hotel, beaches and jet skis, neon nightlife, and the social-media inserts that made Trailer 1 famous. Highway signs in Trailer 1 also name districts — Stockyard, Catalan Blvd and Kelly County — alongside Downtown.

One caution: those district names come from on-screen signage spotted by viewers, not from an official district list. Rockstar has not published a Vice City district map, so treat the names as seen, not as confirmed geography.

03

Beyond the city

Trailer 2's big reveal was how much of Leonida sits outside Vice City. Five places, five very different moods.

Leonida Keys

Causeway bridges, stilt houses and marinas — and Brian's Boat Works, where Jason's story begins. This is the laid-back, sun-bleached end of the state, and Jason's home turf in the trailers.

Grassrivers

Airboats through sawgrass, alligators in the water, and not much else for miles. The trailers use it for the wildest, most Florida moments — and it is where the map stops feeling like a city at all.

Port Gellhorn

The forgotten coast: cheap motels, shuttered tourist attractions and empty strip malls, sitting next to new opportunities. It appears repeatedly in Trailer 2, including the closing shots.

Ambrosia

A small inland town of old-school storefronts — rural Leonida, far from the beach and the neon. One of the four new names Trailer 2's marketing introduced.

Mount Kalaga National Park

Forested hills against the state's northern border, with hunting, fishing and off-road trails. Little of it appears in the trailers themselves — most of what we know comes from the screenshots released alongside them.

Watson Bay Community-named

A coastal spot identified by community map-builders in Trailer 2 footage. Rockstar has not highlighted it directly, so treat it as community-identified — not official.

04

What we don't know yet

The honest part of any GTA 6 map page: the map itself has not been published.

No official map — and no official size.

Rockstar has not published a full map or an exact size figure, and has not confirmed that every location in the trailers is freely explorable. Since then, co-studio head Rob Nelson has given size comparisons — about twice GTA 5 and three times Red Dead Redemption 2 — detailed in the next section. Fan-made maps built from trailer analysis — and from leaked material — are detailed guesses, however good they look. Do not quote their measurements as fact.

Reports say the full map of Leonida will be offered as a free download from launch day, November 19, 2026 — that is reported, not confirmed on Rockstar's own pages as of this writing. When the real map arrives, this page will be updated.

How big is Leonida, really?

Rockstar has published no exact size — but its own developers have given comparisons. Here they are, with the honest caveats.

ComparisonWhat was saidWho said it
Leonida vs GTA 5’s mapAbout twice the sizeRob Nelson, Rockstar North co-studio head, to press and creators at the post-Extended Look previews (Aug–Sep 2026); Aaron Garbut, co-studio head, to Game Informer (Sep 2026 cover story)
Leonida vs Red Dead Redemption 2About three times the sizeRob Nelson; also Rockstar to The New York Times
Vice City vs Los SantosAbout twice the sizeRob Nelson; also Rockstar to The New York Times
Built-up towns outside the city11 times GTA 5’sRob Nelson to TroisCouleurs — about the towns, not the whole map

Three honest caveats. First, these are comparisons, not measurements — Rockstar has given no figure in square kilometres or square miles. Second, community measurements of the leaked map put Leonida’s land at roughly 83–86 km², against about 75–80 km² measured for GTA 5 — so the “twice” figure likely counts water and out-of-bounds areas, not drivable land alone. Third, the “11 times” figure is the one that gets misquoted: it counts the built-up towns outside Vice City, not the whole map. Any site that gives you an exact square-kilometre figure for Leonida is giving you an estimate, not a Rockstar number.

06

Map questions, answered

What players actually ask about the world of GTA 6.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar has not published an exact size, but co-studio head Rob Nelson has said Leonida is about twice the size of GTA 5’s map and three times Red Dead Redemption 2’s, with Vice City alone about twice Los Santos. Those are comparisons, not measurements — no official square-kilometre figure exists, and any exact number you see is an estimate.

Is the whole game set in Vice City?

No. Vice City is the main city, but the game spans the whole fictional state of Leonida — the Keys, the Grassrivers wetlands, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park are all officially named regions.

Can you visit the Leonida Keys?

The Keys are a named region on Rockstar's official tour, and Jason lives there in the trailers — so expect to spend real time there, not just see it in a cutscene. How much of it is explorable is not confirmed.

When will we see the full map?

Reports say Rockstar will offer the full map as a free download from launch day, November 19, 2026. That is reported, not confirmed — and until it appears, no map image on the internet is official.

Is Vice City the same as in GTA: Vice City (2002)?

Same city, new version. This is a modern, much larger Vice City built for GTA VI — a return to the setting, not a remake of the 2002 game.
