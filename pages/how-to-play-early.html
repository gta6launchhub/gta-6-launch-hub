01

Why New Zealand plays first

GTA 6 does not unlock everywhere at once. It unlocks at midnight local time in each store region — so the clock reaches New Zealand first.

Los Angeles, United StatesThu Nov 19, 12:00 AM PSTThu Nov 19, 8:00 AM

New Zealand’s midnight lands 18 hours before New York’s and 21 hours before Los Angeles’. That gap is the whole trick. The key point: the unlock follows the store region, not where you are sitting — on Xbox that region follows the console’s location setting, on PlayStation it follows the PSN account’s country. The full 31-city unlock table is on the Launch Hub home page.

02

Xbox: how the trick works

On Xbox, the unlock is tied to the console’s location setting — so players change the setting, not the account.

  1. Buy the digital game and finish the pre-load. The pre-load opened November 10. The trick does nothing without the game already downloaded — up to 212 GB of it. See the pre-load guide.
  2. On your Xbox, open Settings → System → Language & location and set the location to New Zealand.
  3. Restart the console so the new location takes effect.
  4. At New Zealand midnight — 11:00 AM UTC on November 18 (6:00 AM ET / 3:00 AM PT) — the game should unlock, up to 18 hours before the US East Coast unlock.

Why it works: for digital releases with a rolling midnight unlock, Xbox checks the console’s location setting. That is how the trick has worked for other games. Microsoft has not confirmed it works for GTA 6 specifically, and Rockstar has announced no early-access window — so treat it as a workaround that usually works, not a guarantee.

03

PlayStation 5: why it is different

On PS5 there is no location setting that changes the unlock. The unlock follows the country of the PSN account that bought the game.

Changing the console’s language, time zone or clock does nothing. The only way to play at New Zealand midnight on PS5 is a separate New Zealand PSN account — and a second copy of the game bought from the New Zealand PlayStation Store, which usually means paying with a New Zealand payment method or gift card. Your saves stay on that second account; they do not move to your main one. For most players, that is a lot of money and hassle for 18 hours.

The boxed copy does not help either: the box contains a download code, not a disc, and redeeming it ties the game to your account’s region.

04

The honest limits

Before you switch regions, the five things that are usually left out of the hype.

  • Not endorsed by Rockstar
    Rockstar has announced no early-access window and has not endorsed any workaround. This is a community trick, not an official path.
  • It could stop working
    If Rockstar or Microsoft tie the unlock to the purchasing account instead of the console location, the Xbox trick does nothing. It has worked for other games; it is not confirmed for this one.
  • The ban risk is unconfirmed
    Some reports warn that switching regions could draw account action; others say there is none. Neither side has published proof either way — treat the risk as unconfirmed, not zero and not certain.
  • It is not a demo or a beta
    There is no early access, beta or demo for GTA 6. The trick only moves the unlock hour — the game itself is the same full game.
  • Pre-load still required
    Without the full download finished — up to 212 GB — an early unlock buys you nothing but a download screen.
05

Playing early, answered

The questions everyone asks about the New Zealand trick.

Can I play GTA 6 early?

In one sense, yes: New Zealand unlocks first, at 11:00 AM UTC on November 18. On Xbox, switching the console location to New Zealand can bring the unlock forward by up to 18 hours. On PS5, only a separate New Zealand account and a second purchase does it.

Does the New Zealand trick work on Xbox?

That is how it has worked for other rolling-midnight releases: Xbox checks the console’s location setting. Microsoft has not confirmed it works for GTA 6 specifically, so it is a workaround that usually works — not a guarantee.

Does it work on PlayStation 5?

Not on your main account. The unlock follows the PSN account’s store country, so changing the console’s language or clock does nothing. You would need a separate New Zealand PSN account and a second copy from the New Zealand store.

Will I get banned for switching regions?

No confirmed case has been published. Some reports warn of a possible risk, others say there is none — the risk is unconfirmed either way.

Do I need to re-download the game?

No. The pre-load is the full game; the trick changes when it unlocks, not what is installed.

Does it work with a physical copy?

No. The boxed copy is a download code, not a disc, and redeeming it ties the game to your account’s region — there is no disc to move between regions.
